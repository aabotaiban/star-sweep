STAR SWEEP
==========

Fly your saucer through a falling sky. Catch the stars, dodge or blast the meteors.
Big meteors break into fragments when shot. The sky speeds up every 20 seconds.

HOW TO START
  Double-click StarSweep.exe. Nothing to install.
  The first time, the game opens its How to Play guide (7 short pages).

  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the game is new and not
  code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

CONTROLS
  W / Up arrow ........ fly forward (where the nose points)
  S / Down arrow ...... fly backward
  A D / Left Right .... slide sideways
  Mouse ............... the ship turns to face the pointer
  Q / E ............... turn without a mouse
  Space / left click .. fire the laser (hold)
  P or Esc ............ pause
  H ................... How to Play guide
  M ................... sound on/off

SCORING
  Star .............. 10 x your combo (every 5 stars in a row raises it, up to x5)
  Meteor shot ....... +15     Fragment shot ... +10
  Ram with shield ... +5
  Power-ups: heart = extra life, hexagon = shield (6 s), clock = slow-mo (5 s)

  The laser heats up: about 3 seconds of non-stop fire overheats it until it cools.

Your best score is saved in %APPDATA%\StarSweep\highscore.json.
Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
