## Description (paste this)

**Fly. Aim. Blast. Catch the stars.**

Star Sweep is a fast little arcade game for Windows. Pilot a silver saucer through a sky full of falling stars and tumbling meteors. Catch stars to build your combo, swing your ship around to laser the rocks in your way, and see how long you can last as the sky speeds up.

**Features**

- **Free flight:** fly forward, back and sideways; your ship turns to face your mouse
- **Laser cannon:** blast meteors from any angle, but watch the heat bar or it overheats
- **Shattering meteors:** big rocks break into fragments that fly apart, still dangerous
- **Combos:** catch stars in a row to multiply your score up to ×5
- **Power-ups:** extra lives, a ram-anything shield, and slow-motion
- **Built-in guide:** a 7-page illustrated How to Play opens on your first launch
- Retro synth sound effects, and your best score is saved

**Controls**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| W / S or ↑ / ↓ | fly forward / backward |
| A / D or ← / → | slide sideways |
| Mouse (or Q / E) | turn the ship |
| Space or left click (hold) | fire |
| P | pause |
| H | How to Play |
| M | sound on/off |

**Install:** download the zip, unzip it, and double-click `StarSweep.exe`. Nothing to install.
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game is new and not code-signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

## Steps

1. Sign up or log in at itch.io, then go to **Dashboard → Create new project**.
2. Fill in the fields from the table above.
3. Under **Uploads**, upload `StarSweep-windows.zip` and tick **Windows**.
4. Upload `cover.png` as the cover image and the four screenshots.
5. Set visibility to **Draft**, click **Save & view page**, and check how it looks.
6. When you're happy, set visibility to **Public** and save. Your game is live.

To update the game later: rebuild `StarSweep.exe`, make a new zip the same way, and upload it in place of the old one on the same page.
